Citrusdal → Tulbagh

9-day farm-to-farm hike (8 walking days + 1 rest day) from Piekenierskloof Pass over the Koue Bokkeveld to the NG Kerk in Tulbagh.

Waymarks

Follow white signs with a red lighthouse and a red arrow. Some stretches also use red arrows painted on rocks.

Day by day

Good to know

  • Private farmland: stay on the marked route. Don't pick fruit or damage trees, close every gate behind you, and don't disturb the people who live there.
  • Road sections: parts of Days 1, 2, 3, 6, 8 and 9 follow tar roads, including the Middelberg Pass, which has heavy trucks. Walk on the shoulder or on the gravel beside the tar, and wear something bright.
  • Bees: there are beehives in the orchards on Day 6.
  • Water and food: stops are far apart, so carry enough water for the whole day. Dried fruit is for sale at Kunje (Day 3; ask at the office). Sip Café at Slagboom is the end of Day 8.
  • Easy places to go wrong: Day 6 at the old empty earth dam (go right and find the cycle path). Day 8: do not take the footpath around the power station, and do not take the left footpath when you hear the tar road. Day 7: do not turn right to Op-die-Berg.
  • Weather: the Koue Bokkeveld gets very cold in winter and can have snow. Summers are hot and dry with little shade on the orchard roads.
  • Getting home: the organisers can arrange transport from Tulbagh back to Citrusdal (see the booklet).
  • Contacts: phone numbers for the hosts and farm owners are in the printed booklet. They are left off this public page on purpose.
